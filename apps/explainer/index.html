One ML-KEM exchange, step by step

ML-KEM is the post-quantum key-encapsulation mechanism NIST standardised in August 2024 (it was called Kyber during the competition). This page runs one exchange in your browser with latticework, a from-scratch TypeScript implementation, and draws the actual numbers at every step. Every figure comes from values recorded while the real functions ran.

latticework is unaudited and written for learning. JavaScript gives no constant-time guarantees: the engine decides how long a comparison or a modulo takes, and this code makes no claim to resist timing or cache side channels. For anything real, use an audited implementation.
